# Trinity OS Contributor Copyright Assignment Agreement

**Version 1.0 — October 2026**

This Contributor Copyright Assignment Agreement (“Agreement”) is between the individual or entity identified in the signature block (“Contributor”) and James Davis (“Project Owner”), for contributions proposed for inclusion in Trinity OS (“Contribution”).

This document is intended to be signed before a Contribution is incorporated into the official Trinity OS project. Submission of a pull request or other material alone does not constitute acceptance of this Agreement.

Upon acceptance of a Contribution into the official Trinity OS project, Contributor hereby irrevocably assigns to Project Owner all worldwide right, title, and interest that Contributor owns in the copyright and copyright-related rights in the accepted Contribution, including the rights to reproduce, prepare derivative works, distribute, publicly display, publicly perform, license, sublicense, relicense, enforce, and otherwise exploit the accepted Contribution for the full term of those rights and any renewals or extensions.

If applicable law prevents assignment of a particular copyright-related right, Contributor grants Project Owner an exclusive, perpetual, irrevocable, worldwide, transferable, sublicensable, royalty-free license to exercise that right to the maximum extent permitted by law.

To the maximum extent permitted by applicable law, Contributor waives and agrees not to assert moral rights or similar author rights that would prevent Project Owner from modifying, licensing, relicensing, distributing, or otherwise using the accepted Contribution. This does not prohibit reasonable authorship credit.

Contributor grants Project Owner and recipients authorized by Project Owner a perpetual, worldwide, nonexclusive, transferable, sublicensable, royalty-free patent license under patent claims owned or controlled by Contributor that are necessarily infringed by the accepted Contribution alone or by its combination with Trinity as submitted by Contributor. No ownership of Contributor patents is transferred by this Section.

Contributor represents that:

(a) Contributor has the legal authority to enter this Agreement and make the assignment and grants stated here;

This Agreement does not transfer ownership of third-party material that Contributor does not own. Contributor must identify any third-party material and its applicable license before acceptance. Project Owner may reject any Contribution for licensing, provenance, technical, or other reasons.

## 6. Project Owner's Rights

Project Owner may use, modify, combine, distribute, license, sublicense, relicense, commercialize, discontinue, or decline to use an accepted Contribution. Project Owner has no obligation to accept, maintain, or distribute any Contribution.

## 7. Contributor Credit

Where reasonably practical, the project may preserve authorship credit. Nothing in this Agreement requires a particular form, placement, or duration of credit unless separately agreed in writing.

## 8. No Compensation

Unless separately agreed in writing, Contributor submits the Contribution voluntarily and receives no payment, royalty, ownership interest, or commercial license in exchange for the Contribution.

This Agreement is the complete agreement concerning the copyright assignment for the Contribution unless a separate written agreement signed by both Contributor and Project Owner states otherwise. Changes must be in writing.

If any provision is unenforceable, it shall be enforced to the maximum extent permitted and the remaining provisions remain in effect.
