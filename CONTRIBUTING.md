# Contributing to Trinity OS

Contributions are welcome for the purpose of improving Trinity OS. Trinity uses centralized copyright ownership so that the official project has a clear chain of title and only the Copyright Owner can alter Trinity's licensing terms.

## Before a contribution can be accepted

A contributor must execute the current `CONTRIBUTOR_COPYRIGHT_ASSIGNMENT_AGREEMENT.md` (or another written agreement expressly approved by the Copyright Owner). Opening an issue, posting code, or submitting a pull request by itself does **not** transfer copyright and does **not** guarantee acceptance.

## What contributors represent

Contributors must have the legal right to submit their work. Do not submit code copied from incompatible projects, confidential employer material, proprietary third-party code, or material whose license prevents the required assignment. Third-party material must be clearly identified with its provenance and license.

## Licensing of accepted contributions

Once a contribution is accepted pursuant to a signed assignment agreement, the assigned copyright becomes part of the official Trinity project and is distributed under the Trinity OS license selected by the Copyright Owner.

Contributors retain credit for their authorship. Copyright assignment is about ownership and licensing authority; it does not erase attribution.

## Commercial rights

Submitting a contribution does not grant the contributor commercial rights to Trinity. Commercial use requires separate written authorization from the Copyright Owner.
